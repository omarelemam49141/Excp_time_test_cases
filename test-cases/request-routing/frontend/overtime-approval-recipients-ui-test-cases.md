## Scope

What a tester sees after the overtime approval routing fix and the approval inbox fix: every Pending overtime request reaches the employee's direct leader (`LeaderId`) and the employee's primary organizational-structure parent; either one can accept or reject alone; a leader who cannot decide a request no longer sees it in the approval inbox; notification-only leaders are told but cannot act; requests created by End work, attendance and overtime edits and rebuilds get their approvers and notify them once.

Use two browsers (or private windows) for two leaders. Names below: **Emp** (employee), **LeaderA** (Emp's direct leader in the user profile), **LeaderB** (a different leader who is Emp's parent in Company structure), **Stranger** (a leader unrelated to Emp), **Admin**.

## Test scenarios

- End work with overtime creates a request that the direct leader can see and accept.
- A different organizational-structure parent also sees it and can accept it.
- When both leaders can act, the first decision finishes the request; no second approval is needed.
- When the parent and the direct leader are the same person, the request is listed once.
- A direct leader set to "Notification only" on the team (not on the person) does not see the request in the approval inbox and cannot accept it.
- An unrelated leader does not see the request; Admin still sees every request in the general view.
- A leader who is only the profile leader of a request routed to someone else does not see it.
- With a final approver configured, other receivers see the row, but Accept is disabled and only the final approver can accept.
- Attendance/overtime edits and rebuilds replace the request with a new one that has its approvers and notifications.
- The employee's own lists are unchanged.
