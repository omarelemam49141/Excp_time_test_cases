# Quick Entry clarity pass, side panel, and compact days test cases (frontend)

## Metadata

- Project: `excp-time`
- Feature: `quick-entry`
- Layer: `frontend`
- Routes: `/attendance/quick-entry`
- APIs: `POST /api/attendance-record/Get-bulk-details-in-range`
- Related code: `src/app/features/attendance/components/attendance-quick-entry/attendance-quick-entry.component.html`, `src/app/features/attendance/components/attendance-quick-entry/attendance-quick-entry.component.scss`, `src/app/features/attendance/components/attendance-quick-entry/attendance-quick-entry.component.ts`
- Related docs: `docs/frontend/technical/quick-entry-clarity-and-side-panel.md`

## Scope

Validate the simplified upper section, the collapsible side panel (rail, actions, navigators, tools, help), the compact one-line day rows, newest-first order with day-1 navigation, landing-day rules, and the inline load-error card with Retry.

## Test pages

- `/attendance/quick-entry`

## Test scenarios

- The header shows the title and a Period button that opens a floating date dropdown; the totals are a separate section below.
- Save, reload, navigators, tools, admin target, and help live in one side panel that stays out of the way when collapsed.
- Each day is one compact row; leave actions and hints appear when the day is opened.
- The list is newest first while jump buttons and the tour still run from day 1.
- Opening the page or picking a period lands on the newest day without expanding anything.
- Unsaved state follows real changes only: adding then removing a break or an overtime draft leaves the day clean.
- A rate-limited or failed load shows one clear card with Retry.
