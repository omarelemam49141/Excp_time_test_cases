## Scope

- The "cover hours" request was removed. Missed time is worked out automatically from the work schedule, paid back automatically by short extra time under the overtime gap, and then covered by approved overtime. Overtime is the only extra-time request.
- Month balance, hours report cover, the after-gap decision (end of work and overtime session), approval scope, the Team Lead balance dashboard with adjustments, and the Leader attendance roster.

## Test pages

- API only (no screen). The automated tests run on an in-memory SQLite database.

## Test scenarios

- Required hours skip holidays and approved leave only
- Short extra time under the gap pays back missed time silently
- Pending overtime never changes the balance; approved overtime covers the missed time first
- The hours report covers missed time from approved overtime and pays only the rest
- After-gap decision for the overtime session and for end of work
- Old clients and old cover data keep working
- Leader, HR and Admin overtime approval scope
- Team Lead balance dashboard and adjustments
- Leader attendance roster scope
