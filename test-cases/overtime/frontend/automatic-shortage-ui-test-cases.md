# Automatic shortage and Overtime-only (frontend) Test Cases

## Metadata

- Project: `excp-time`
- Feature: `overtime`
- Layer: `frontend`
- Routes: `/home`, `/attendance/overtime-timer`, `/admin-leader/list-requests`, `/admin-leader/working-hours-balance`, `/admin-leader/team-attendance`, `/admin/reports/employee-hours-report`
- APIs: `GET /api/attendance-overtime/Month-shortage-balance`, `POST /api/attendance-overtime/End-overtime`, `POST /api/AttendanceRecord/End-work`, `GET /api/WorkingHoursBalance/team`, `POST /api/WorkingHoursBalance/adjust`, `POST /api/Reports/users-daily-attendance-roster`, `POST /api/Reports/employee-hours-report`
- Related code: `src/app/pages/home/home.component.ts`, `src/app/core/models/month-work-balance.model.ts`, `src/app/features/admin-leader/shared/services/over-time.service.ts`
- Related docs: `docs/frontend/technical/working-hours-month-card-and-after-gap-decision.md`, `docs/frontend/technical/working-hours-balance-dashboard.md`, `docs/frontend/technical/users-daily-attendance-roster-page.md`, `docs/frontend/technical/employee-hours-report-automatic-cover.md`
- Automation: -

## Scope

- The Home month card, the after-gap decision when an overtime session or the working day ends, Leader approval of overtime, the Team Lead working hours balance page, team attendance, HR access, the automatic cover block of the Employee hours report, and Arabic and English layout.
- Cover hours no longer exist as a request. Overtime is the only extra-time request. The old cover screens are retired in `test-cases/overtime/frontend/cover-shortage-session-ui-test-cases.md`.
- Backend rules are in `test-cases/overtime/backend/automatic-shortage-test-cases.md`.

## Test pages

- `/home`, `/attendance/overtime-timer`
- `/admin-leader/list-requests`, `/admin-leader/working-hours-balance`, `/admin-leader/team-attendance`
- `/admin/reports/employee-hours-report`

## Test scenarios

- Month card figures and no cover hours anywhere on Home
- Overtime session and end work decision under, at and above the gap
- Leader accept and refuse, missed time covered after approval
- Balance page: signed balances, search, adjust limits, roles
- Team attendance for Leader, HR and Admin
- HR only access and Employee only redirect
- Report block with automatic cover
- Arabic and English layout at 1366x768
